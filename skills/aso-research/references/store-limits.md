# Store metadata limits (re-check before release)
Written from public store documentation; stores change them. Verify on the current Play Console / App Store Connect help page.

| Field | Google Play | Apple App Store |
|---|---|---|
| App name / title | 30 characters | 30 characters |
| Short description / subtitle | 80 characters (short description) | 30 characters (subtitle) |
| Full description | 4000 characters | 4000 characters (description, not indexed for search) |
| Keyword field | none (Play indexes title, short and full description) | 100 characters, comma-separated |
| Promotional text | - | 170 characters |

Notes: Play ranks on text in title, short and full description; Apple indexes title, subtitle and the keyword field, so repeating a word across them wastes space. Play policy forbids misleading, keyword-stuffed or emoji-heavy titles and unverifiable claims ("#1", "best").
