# Headlines
- 2-4 words; max ~22 characters per line, 2 lines. A verb or an outcome: "Unlock the next twist", "Watch in 90 seconds".
- Benefit over feature ("Never miss an episode" beats "Notifications").
- Use the words people search (from keyword research) in frames 1-3.
- One emphasised word allowed (colour, highlight box, or underline), never more.
- Subline optional: 3-6 words that make the headline concrete ("New episodes every day").
- Tone matches the category: playful for consumer, calm and precise for finance/health.
- Localise per market, not by literal translation; re-check length in German and line breaks in Arabic (RTL mirrors the layout).
- No claims you cannot prove ("#1", "best", "free forever"); stores and users punish them.
