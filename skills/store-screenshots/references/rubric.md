# Rubric (score 1-5, look at the contact sheet at thumbnail size, ~200 px wide per frame)
1. **1-second test:** frame 1 alone - can a stranger say what the app does?
2. **Headline legibility** at thumbnail size; contrast.
3. **Proof per frame:** does the UI fragment prove that frame's headline?
4. **Set cohesion:** one font, colour field, framing; panorama links feel intended.
5. **Distinctiveness:** would it stand out next to 5 competitors' sets (check with aso-research listings)?
6. **Honesty:** real UI, real numbers, labelled concept if pre-launch.
Ship at >= 4 on 1-3 and 6. Fix the lowest-scoring frame first.
